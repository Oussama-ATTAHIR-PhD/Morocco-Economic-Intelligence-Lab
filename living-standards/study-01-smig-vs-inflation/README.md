# Study 01

# Has Morocco's Minimum Wage Protected Workers Against Inflation Since 2000?

---

## Research Question

Has Morocco's statutory minimum wage (SMIG) effectively protected Moroccan workers against inflation between 2000 and 2024?

---

## Objective

This study evaluates whether increases in Morocco's statutory minimum wage have been sufficient to preserve workers' purchasing power over the last twenty-five years.

The analysis compares annual changes in the statutory monthly minimum wage (SMIG) with the Consumer Price Index (CPI).

---

## Period

2000–2024

---

## Country

Morocco

---

## Data Sources

- World Bank
- Ministry of Economic Inclusion, Small Business, Employment and Skills

---

## Main Indicators

- Consumer Price Index (CPI)
- Monthly SMIG
