# Methodology

## 1. Objective

The objective of this study is to analyse the evolution of Moroccan household purchasing power between 2007 and 2024.

The analysis focuses on the household institutional sector (S.14).

The study distinguishes between nominal economic growth and real economic growth after accounting for inflation.

---

## 2. Study Period

The study covers the period:

2007–2024

The selected period is determined by the availability and harmonisation of the household sector data and consumer price index series used in the analysis.

---

## 3. Unit of Analysis

The unit of analysis is the household institutional sector:

S.14

The results therefore represent macroeconomic aggregates for Moroccan households.

---

## 4. Data Sources

The data were obtained from the High Commission for Planning of Morocco (HCP).

The main variables used are:

- Consumer Price Index
- Household disposable income
- Household final consumption expenditure

The study also relies on national accounts data related to the household institutional sector.

---

## 5. Variables

### Consumer Price Index

The Consumer Price Index is used to measure changes in the general price level.

The CPI series is harmonized using a base year of:

2017 = 100

---

### Disposable Income

Disposable income represents the income available to households after the distribution and redistribution of income.

The variable is expressed in millions of Moroccan dirhams.

---

### Household Consumption

Household final consumption expenditure represents the expenditure of households on goods and services.

The variable is expressed in millions of Moroccan dirhams.

---

## 6. Real Disposable Income

Real disposable income is calculated by adjusting nominal disposable income for inflation.

The formula is:

Real Disposable Income = Nominal Disposable Income / CPI × 100

---

## 7. Real Consumption

Real consumption is calculated by adjusting nominal household consumption for inflation.

The formula is:

Real Consumption = Nominal Consumption / CPI × 100

---

## 8. Inflation

The annual inflation rate is calculated using the percentage change in the Consumer Price Index.

The formula is:

Inflation = ((CPI_t / CPI_(t-1)) - 1) × 100

---

## 9. Real Disposable Income Growth

The annual growth rate of real disposable income is calculated as:

Real Income Growth =
((Real Income_t / Real Income_(t-1)) - 1) × 100

---

## 10. Household Saving Rate

The household saving rate is calculated as:

Saving Rate =
((Disposable Income - Consumption) / Disposable Income) × 100

---

## 11. Analytical Approach

The analysis follows five stages:

1. Data quality verification
2. Variable construction
3. Exploratory analysis
4. Economic interpretation
5. Statistical summary and export

The analysis focuses primarily on:

- Long-term growth
- Inflationary episodes
- Real income dynamics
- Consumption dynamics
- Saving behaviour

---

## 12. Interpretation

The results should be interpreted as macroeconomic indicators.

An increase in aggregate real household income does not necessarily imply that all households experienced the same improvement.

Differences may exist across:

- Income groups
- Regions
- Household types
- Employment categories

The study therefore measures the evolution of the household sector as a whole.
