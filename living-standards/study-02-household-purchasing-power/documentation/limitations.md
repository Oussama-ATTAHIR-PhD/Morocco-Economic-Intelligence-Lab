# Study Limitations

## Aggregate Analysis

This study analyses macroeconomic aggregates for the household institutional sector.

The results therefore do not describe the experience of every individual household.

---

## Distributional Differences

The analysis does not measure differences between:

- Low-income households
- Middle-income households
- High-income households

---

## Geographic Differences

The study does not distinguish between:

- Urban households
- Rural households
- Regions

---

## Household Composition

The analysis does not measure differences according to:

- Household size
- Employment status
- Age
- Household composition

---

## Aggregate Purchasing Power

An improvement in aggregate real disposable income does not necessarily mean that all households experienced the same improvement in purchasing power.

Some groups may have experienced stronger or weaker outcomes.

---

## Future Research

Future studies could extend the analysis using:

- Household survey microdata
- Income distribution data
- Regional data
- Household expenditure structure
- Food and energy price dynamics
