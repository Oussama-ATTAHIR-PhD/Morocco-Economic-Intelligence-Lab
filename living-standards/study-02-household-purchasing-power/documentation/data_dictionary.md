# Data Dictionary

## year

Calendar year.

Type: Integer

Study period: 2007–2024

---

## cpi_base_2017_harmonized

Harmonized Consumer Price Index.

Base year:

2017 = 100

Used to calculate inflation and deflate nominal variables.

---

## disposable_income_s14_harmonized

Gross disposable income of the household institutional sector.

Institutional sector:

S.14

Unit:

Millions of Moroccan dirhams.

---

## consumption_s14_harmonized

Household final consumption expenditure.

Unit:

Millions of Moroccan dirhams.

---

## real_disposable_income

Inflation-adjusted household disposable income.

Calculated as:

Nominal Disposable Income / CPI × 100

---

## real_consumption

Inflation-adjusted household consumption.

Calculated as:

Nominal Consumption / CPI × 100

---

## inflation

Annual percentage change in the Consumer Price Index.

Unit:

Percent.

---

## real_disposable_income_growth

Annual percentage change in real disposable income.

Unit:

Percent.

---

## saving_rate

Household saving rate.

Calculated as:

((Disposable Income - Consumption) / Disposable Income) × 100

Unit:

Percent.
