# Key Findings

## Long-Term Price Evolution

Between 2007 and 2024, the Consumer Price Index increased by 36.96%.

This indicates a substantial increase in the general price level over the study period.

---

## Nominal Income Growth

Household disposable income increased by 123.91% between 2007 and 2024.

This represents a strong increase in nominal household resources.

---

## Nominal Consumption Growth

Household consumption increased by 127.60% over the same period.

Nominal consumption therefore increased slightly faster than nominal disposable income.

---

## Real Income Growth

After adjusting for inflation, real disposable income increased by 63.48%.

This indicates substantial long-term growth in the real resources of the household sector.

---

## Real Consumption Growth

Real household consumption increased by 66.18%.

Real consumption therefore followed a broadly positive long-term trajectory.

---

## Major Economic Shocks

The largest decline in real disposable income occurred in 2020.

Real disposable income growth reached:

-4.35%

The strongest real income growth occurred in 2021:

+8.09%

---

## Inflation Shock

The highest inflation rate occurred in 2022:

6.64%

During the same year, real disposable income declined despite nominal income growth.

This demonstrates the importance of distinguishing between nominal income growth and real purchasing power.

---

## Saving Behaviour

The household saving rate reached its highest level in 2020:

15.20%

The lowest saving rate was observed in 2023:

10.54%

The results indicate a significant change in household financial behaviour during the post-pandemic period.

---

## Overall Conclusion

The long-term evolution of Moroccan household purchasing power was positive.

However, the trajectory was not linear.

The household sector remained exposed to economic shocks and inflationary pressures.
